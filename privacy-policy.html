Privacy Policy

Welcome to RankIt ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. By using RankIt, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Display name (chosen during onboarding or from your social account)
  • Country of residence (chosen during onboarding)
  • Email address (if you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook)

Information collected automatically

  • Game scores, round results, and gameplay statistics
  • Device push notification token (for game invites and streak reminders)
  • App usage data (anonymous, for improving the experience)

Contacts (optional)

If you choose to find friends via your phone contacts, we access your contact list solely to match phone numbers against our user base. Phone numbers are immediately converted to a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) — the original numbers are never stored or transmitted.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display leaderboards and rankings
  • To send push notifications (streak reminders, game invites) — only with your permission
  • To show relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To process in-app subscriptions via RevenueCat
  • To improve gameplay, fix bugs, and enhance the app experience

3. Third-Party Services

RankIt uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and analytics
    firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob — advertising
    policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
    revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Facebook Login — optional social sign-in
    facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Expo (notifications) — push notification delivery
    expo.dev/privacy

4. Advertising & Tracking

RankIt displays ads to free users via Google AdMob. On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before serving personalized ads. If you decline, we serve non-personalized ads only.

Premium subscribers see no advertisements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share data only:

  • With service providers listed above, to operate the app
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

Your display name, country flag, and game scores are visible to other players on leaderboards. Your email address is never shown publicly.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

RankIt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@rankit.app.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), Firebase Security Rules, and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date below and, where appropriate, through in-app notification. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
